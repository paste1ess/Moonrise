# Moonrise 🌙 

A native, lightweight, *fast* local music player designed entirely for modern Windows.

<a href="https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n53pcdpl7wx?referrer=appbadge&mode=full" target="_blank"  rel="noopener noreferrer">

</a>

> [!WARNING]
> Moonrise is currently unfinished, standing in a beta-like state even while it's released. There's still so many features to be added and refined! Bug reports are always welcome!

Moonrise is the spiritual successor to our previous project, Musium, built from the ground up to improve on every aspect. **Better** performance, **lower** resource usage, **more** features, and fewer bugs.

---

## Features

### Synced Lyrics
Synchronized line-by-line lyrics sourced locally or via LRCLIB, with support for the `.lrc` format.

<img alt="synchronized lyrics" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a1482cd-c8f8-4cdc-9873-df3a8a246b92" />

### Mica+
A custom dynamic background that subtly reacts to your music, *without* tanking performance or battery life.

<img alt="mica-plus" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aee965a5-35db-4cd0-89b8-c27bf63aeb3f" />

### Playlists
Support for importing and playing existing `.m3u` and `.m3u8` playlists. Playlist creation and in-app editing are planned for later versions.

<img alt="test playlist screen" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b9ac1f0b-2d76-4eea-bef4-dbf826193d50" />

### Discord Integration
Moonrise integrates with Discord to let your friends know what songs you're currently listening to through your status.

## Other improvements

Moonrise includes countless improvements over Musium, like:
* **Better start up times** - Moonrise starts and is ready to use faster than Musium, so you can get to your music as fast as possible.
* **Resource usage optimizations** - Moonrise optimizes its resource usage, keeping things quick and snappy when needed, and scaling back when you want maximum multitasking performance.
* **Artists** - Full support for artists, allowing you to shuffle your favorite band's discography, combined with official artist images retrieved from the public iTunes API.
